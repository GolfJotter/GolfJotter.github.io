GolfJotter Privacy Policy

GolfJotter uses your information to create scorecards, link fourballs, manage competitions, save player details, and manage golf course information.

Information We Collect

GolfJotter may collect your name, email address, player names, golf course details, handicaps, scores, linked scorecard information, competition information, and saved course information.

Location

GolfJotter may ask for access to your device location so it can show and sort nearby golf courses. Location access is optional. Your location is used only for course search and nearby-course features.

How We Use Information

This information is used only to provide GolfJotter features, including scorecards, shared scorecards, leaderboards, saved players, saved courses, nearby course search, and competitions.

Data Storage

Some information is stored locally on your device. Some information is stored using Firebase services so scorecards and competitions can sync between players.

Sharing

GolfJotter does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with other players only where needed for linked scorecards and competitions.

Tracking and Advertising

GolfJotter does not use your personal information or location for advertising or tracking.
